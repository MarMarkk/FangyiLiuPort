The Row

/ Academic Project

A five-page fashion website I designed and built in WordPress, inspired by The Row’s clean, minimal style.

01

Project brief

The final assignment was to create a website on any topic of interest. I chose my favorite fashion brand, The Row, and used its minimal style to guide the typography, color palette, imagery and page layouts.

I redesigned the homepage, organized brand-related content across five pages and customized the footer to create a consistent experience throughout the site.

02

Website structure

I organized the site into five pages, with relevant text and imagery on each page and a consistent visual style across them.

03

Design & build

I switched the WordPress theme to Inspiro and used Elementor with Elementor Addons by WPZOOM to customize the layouts. I adjusted the typography and color palette to reflect The Row’s understated visual identity.

Customizing the footer and adding social icons helped carry that design through the full site, beyond the homepage.

  • WordPress
  • Elementor
  • Theme customization
  • Visual design
04

Functionality & SEO

Content discovery

I added a Search widget and a fashion and luxury news RSS feed in the footer to connect visitors with relevant content.

Social links & promotion

I added Simple Social Icons and configured Ad Inserter to place an advertisement banner on the Blog page.

On-page SEO

I used Yoast SEO to refine page titles, keywords and readability, applying SEO considerations alongside the visual design.
