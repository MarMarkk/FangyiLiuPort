03

Audience Strategy

MY PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY

I led audience persona development and audience segmentation; contributed to content planning, channel strategy, content concepts, audience-specific messaging and CTA development.

Primary audiences

  • Past and recurring donors
  • Current families and students
  • Volunteers and staff

Secondary audiences

  • Inactive donors
  • First-time contributors
  • Corporate sponsors
  • Boston Marathon runners
  • Community supporters in Boston
  1. Audience Research

    Understand audience relationships, needs and motivations.

  2. Persona & Segmentation

    Organize eight audience groups by their relationship with 3Point, from recurring supporters to people discovering the organization.

  3. Audience Motivation

    Identify why each group would engage, participate or contribute.

  4. Marketing Decision

    Translate insights into messages, content, channels and CTAs that match each audience’s motivation and readiness to act.

Insight → Decision

Insight: Inconsistent communication may cause potential donors to disengage.
Decision: Share recurring impact stories, donor updates and fundraising progress between donation requests.
